MakeWhole refund terms: Aave V3 wstETH CAPO oracle incident, Ethereum, 10 March 2026

PART A. The Aave DAO proposal, quoted verbatim.
Source: https://governance.aave.com/t/direct-to-aip-wsteth-capo-oracle-incident-user-reimbursement/24275 and the AIP payload aave-dao/aave-proposals-v3 commit a93ce7ec2ed3a2df946f6add33b9951bb022d9dc.

"This proposal refunds users who were erroneously liquidated during the wstETH CAPO oracle misconfiguration incident on Ethereum Core and Prime instances. The total refund amounts to 513.19 ETH, with a net cost to the DAO of 358.56 ETH after recoveries, a figure that should decrease as more recoveries are processed."

"A configuration misalignment between the `snapshotRatio` and `snapshotTimestamp` parameters in the wstETH CAPO risk oracle caused the reported wstETH/stETH exchange rate cap to fall approximately 2.85% below the actual market rate. This triggered erroneous liquidations across 34 accounts on the Ethereum Core and Prime instances, totaling ~10,938 wstETH liquidated."

"The affected users bear no responsibility for these liquidations, which were the direct result of a protocol-level configuration error. Making these users whole is a straightforward decision for the DAO and reinforces the trust that underpins Aave's position as the leading lending protocol."

Loss Breakdown table as published (ETH): Oracle profit (loss to users) 382.76; Liquidation bonus (loss to users) 129.72; Goodwill allowance 1; Total loss 513.19; Recovered from Titan Builder (141.60); Recovered liquidation fees (13.32); Net cost to the DAO 358.56.

"Upon AIP execution, the AFC will distribute the appropriate refund amount to each affected user address."

PART B. How this pool applies it: drafted for MakeWhole from the post-mortem — not published by the Aave DAO. The proposal published no per-account formula; the per-wstETH rate and the 1% in [E2] were taken from the Aave Finance Committee's own payout transaction 0x687f2a608fbc48c2f90130fd6f3620835770b03424ed497a5002f095d1c00f3f, which paid 35 accounts although the AIP said 34.

[E1] Eligible: each LiquidationCall event emitted by the Aave V3 Ethereum Core pool 0x87870Bca3F3fD6335C3F4ce8392D69350B4fA4E2 or Prime pool 0x4e033931ad43597d96D6bcc25c280717730B58B1, with wstETH 0x7f39C581F595B53c5cb19bD0b3f8dA6c935E2Ca0 as the collateral, in a successful transaction mined in Ethereum blocks 24626860 to 24628088 inclusive, the blocks in which the capped wstETH price was in force.
[E2] Amount: for each eligible event the refund is the wstETH seized multiplied by 0.034991439125 ETH per wstETH (a rate taken from the DAO's payout transaction), plus 1% of the debt repaid valued in ETH (WETH at 1, cbETH at 1.124931903010748, osETH at 1.067241394153283 ETH per token).
[E3] Payee: the refund is owed to the liquidated borrower address recorded in the event, and is paid to that same address on the payout chain.
[E4] Smart contract wallets: where the borrower address is a single user's own smart contract wallet, such as a DSProxy, whose owner() view returns one externally owned account, the refund is paid to that externally owned account.
[X1] Withheld until shown otherwise: a borrower address that holds contract code on Ethereum, other than an EIP-7702 delegated account, has no key on the payout chain, so its refund is withheld unless an appeal establishes a payee under [E4].
[X2] Excluded: pooled vaults that hold several users' positions, and contracts controlled by more than one key, such as a multisig that needs several signatures, are not a single user's wallet; their refunds are not paid and return to the sponsor.
[X3] Excluded: events outside the block range in [E1], on other pools or chains, with other collateral, in failed transactions, or with a debt asset not priced in [E2]. Each event is refunded once.
[P1] Payment: refunds are paid in GEN at 1 ETH = 0.01 GEN. If the refunds owed exceed the pool, every refund is reduced in the same proportion.
[P2] Deadlines: claims close at the claim deadline and appeals at the appeal deadline recorded with these terms. Whatever is not owed to anyone then returns to the sponsor.
